1. Introduction

INFINEVO TECH PVT LTD ("Company", "we", "our", or "us") operates LeadNex, a business management and CRM platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect, and share information when you access or use our website, application, and related services.

By using LeadNex, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use LeadNex and which features are enabled, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Personal information: first name, last name, email address, phone number, company name, account credentials, and profile details.
  • Business information: customer and contact records, leads, opportunities, communication history, notes, and activity records entered into the platform.
  • Technical information: IP address, browser type, device information, login activity, access timestamps, and diagnostic data.
  • Integration information: information made available when you connect and authorize third-party services.
  • Billing information: subscription status, transaction references, and billing records. Payment card details may be handled by the relevant payment provider.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create, authenticate, and manage user accounts.
  • Provide CRM, lead management, communication, and automation features.
  • Manage contacts, leads, opportunities, and business workflows.
  • Process subscriptions and billing-related requests.
  • Respond to support requests and send service-related communications.
  • Maintain platform security, prevent fraud, and troubleshoot issues.
  • Analyze and improve platform performance and user experience.
  • Comply with applicable laws and enforce our agreements.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

4. Facebook and Meta Data Usage

If you choose to connect a Meta service, such as Facebook or a related business tool, LeadNex may receive information that you authorize through Meta's permission and consent screens. Depending on the permissions granted and the integration used, this may include basic profile information, account or page identifiers, page information, or business information needed to provide the requested feature.

We use information received through Meta integrations only to provide and maintain the functionality you request, provide support, protect the integration, and comply with applicable requirements. We do not sell Meta user data.

You can manage or revoke an application's access through your Meta account settings. Revoking access may stop the connected feature from working. Revoking access does not automatically delete information already held by LeadNex; you may submit a separate deletion request as described below.

Meta's own collection and use of information is governed by Meta's privacy policies and terms.

5. Information Sharing

We may share information when reasonably necessary with:

  • Service providers: hosting, infrastructure, analytics, customer support, communication, and payment providers that help us operate LeadNex.
  • Connected services: third-party platforms you choose to connect and authorize.
  • Your organization: workspace administrators and authorized users, depending on your account and permissions.
  • Legal authorities: where disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, or security.
  • Business successors: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or transfer of business assets, subject to appropriate safeguards.

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

LeadNex and its service providers may use cookies and similar technologies to maintain user sessions, remember preferences, protect accounts, understand service usage, and improve performance. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some features.

7. Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Services, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and protect the platform. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and applicable requirements.

8. User Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You may also withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

If you use LeadNex through an organization, contact your organization's administrator first for requests about workspace or customer data. You may also contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity and authority before acting on a request.

9. Data Deletion

To request deletion of your LeadNex account or personal information, email info@thefinansol.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request. Include the email address associated with your account and describe the information or account you want deleted. Do not include your password.

We will review verified requests and process them within a reasonable period, subject to applicable legal, contractual, and legitimate record-retention requirements. Some information may remain in backups for a limited period before being securely overwritten.

If you connected LeadNex through Facebook or another third-party platform, you may also revoke that platform's access in its account settings. Revoking access does not itself delete information already stored by LeadNex.

10. Third-Party Websites and Services

LeadNex may contain links to third-party websites or integrate with external services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party services. Please review their privacy policies before sharing information with them.

11. Children's Privacy

LeadNex is designed for business use and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact our support team.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, practices, or legal requirements. We will update the effective date when changes are made. Please review this page periodically.
